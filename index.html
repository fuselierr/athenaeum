Two physics-driven page-spreads chained along the spine.
Each spread hinges and fills its own gap with its own air-cushion easing; the two inner pages meet at a hard stop and curl to settle parallel with their cover.
Press R to reset, F to flip the book over, [ / ] to flip through (slides the B/C hinge along the spine). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
