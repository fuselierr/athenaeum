Two independent page-spreads (front: blue/red, back: teal/orange) are chained along the spine.
Each spread hinges and fills its own gap exactly like before, with its own air-cushion easing. The two inner pages (red, teal -- "B" and "C") start together near vertical, slightly apart, not splayed open like the covers -- they can never swing past one another, but that stop has no easing of its own, it's just "can't pass through."
B and C are also moldable: instead of staying flat, each curves from its own hinge to settle parallel with its cover page (A or D), the way a book's inner leaves bend near the spine as it opens.
Press R to reset the drop, F to flip the book over. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
